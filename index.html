Sixteen beta rounds on a real device came before this release, until downloading, trimming, the gallery and the player settled. If you hit a failure, copy its text with “Copy the error” inside the app and post it in the repository issues; that text alone is enough to diagnose it.

الأدوات كلّها داخل الحزمة: yt-dlp وffmpeg وaria2c وبايثون. فلا يطلب التطبيق تثبيت شيء، ولا ينزّل ثنائيّات عند أوّل تشغيل، والعمل كلّه على جهازك بلا خادم وسيط. Every tool ships inside the package: yt-dlp, ffmpeg, aria2c and Python. The app installs nothing, downloads no binaries on first run, and does all the work on your device with no server in between.
