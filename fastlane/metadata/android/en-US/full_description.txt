GMD is a free and open-source media downloader for Android, built on yt-dlp and ffmpeg. Both tools ship inside the package, so nothing is installed and no middleman server ever sees your links.

Features:

* Video and audio downloads at a quality or format of your choosing
* Playlists, each saved into a folder of its own
* Trim a part of a clip — before downloading, or from a file already on your device
* A history of every attempt, with the reason a failed one failed
* A built-in audio player that resumes where you left off
* A gallery of what you downloaded, with sharing and deletion
* Arabic and English, right-to-left aware
* Self-updating: it checks its own releases and installs them
